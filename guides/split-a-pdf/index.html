How to split a PDF into separate files

Split a PDF by selecting the pages you want and exporting them as a new document — free, in your browser, with no upload.

"Splitting" a PDF usually means one of two things: pulling a few pages out into their own file, or breaking one document into several. Both are done the same way here — choose the pages you want to keep, then export.

Steps

  1. Open the editor and drop your PDF onto the page.
  2. In the page thumbnails, click the pages you want in the new file. Selected pages are highlighted; click again to deselect.
  3. Delete the pages you don't want, or keep your selection and go straight to export.
  4. Choose Export → PDF. The result contains only the pages you kept, in the order shown.
  5. To produce a second file from the same original, reload the page and repeat with the other pages.

Reordering while you're there

Pages can be dragged into a different order before exporting, and rotated individually. The exported file reflects exactly what the thumbnails show.

Good to know

  • The original file on your computer is never modified — you always get a new file.
  • Documents up to roughly 100 MB and 500 pages work comfortably. Larger files depend on how much memory your browser has.
  • A password-protected PDF has to be unlocked before it can be edited.
